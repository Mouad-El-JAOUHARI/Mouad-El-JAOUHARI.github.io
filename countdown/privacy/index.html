Privacy Policy for Countdown Widget: Days Until

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use Countdown Widget: Days Until ("the app") by Midnight Dev Studio on Android, and how it is used.

Information the app keeps on your device

Your countdowns (names, dates, icons, colours, clock styles and reminder settings) are saved only on your device so the app, its widgets and its lock-screen wallpaper can show them. If you choose a background photo for the lock screen, a resized copy is stored inside the app on your device. None of this is sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. The app has no accounts and no sign-in.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no information is sent to us to do this.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. This includes a small banner and an optional video you can choose to watch. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • information about ads shown and tapped
  • crash and performance diagnostics for the ads SDK

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings in the app.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings under Ads.
  • You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
